# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can contribute, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and specific in reviews. Explain why, and point at the code or the behaviour.
- Accepting that a maintainer may decline a change, and asking why before arguing.
- Assuming good faith, then checking facts rather than character.
- Respecting creators and their work when discussing data quality. A vlogger is a person, not a
  data source.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or dismissive comments about a person rather than their work.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Dismissing an accessibility or privacy concern because it is inconvenient.
- Sustained disruption of issues or discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, code comments and any space where someone
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact on their GitHub
profile. Reports are read in good faith and kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove
content, or block an account, with an explanation where it is safe to give one.
